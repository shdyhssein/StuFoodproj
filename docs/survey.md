# Survey

Google Forms, anonymous, about 5 minutes. 14 respondents (friends and university groups).
Two versions: ads in order 1-7 (version 1) and reversed (version 2).

Intro: You will see 7 ads for a fictional meal-prep app for students. Please rate each ad honestly.

For each ad (image, headline and text shown):
1. Overall quality of this ad (1-5)
2. Fit for students as a target group (1-5)
3. How likely would you be to click on this ad? (1-5)
4. Does this ad look AI-made? (Yes / No / Not sure)

Optional at the end: how often you plan meals in advance, field of study (field of study not published).
